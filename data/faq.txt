AI Customer Support FAQ

1. How can I reset my password?
Customers can reset their password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login page. They will receive a password-reset link on their registered email address.

2. How can I contact customer support?
Customers can contact support through the support email or by creating a support ticket through the customer portal.

3. What are the customer support hours?
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

4. How can I check my order status?
Customers can check their order status by logging into their account and opening the "My Orders" section.

5. Can I cancel my order?
An order can be cancelled before it is shipped. Once the order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.

6. How can I request a refund?
Customers can submit a refund request through the customer portal. The support team will review the request according to the refund policy.

7. How long does a refund take?
Approved refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

8. How can I update my email address?
Customers can update their email address from the Account Settings section. For security reasons, verification may be required.

9. What should I do if my account is locked?
Customers should contact customer support if their account is locked. The support team will verify the account and help restore access.

10. Does the support agent answer questions outside the company information?
The AI Customer Support Agent is designed to answer questions related to the company's products, services, policies, and support information. It should not answer unrelated general questions.
